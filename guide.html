The Three Fractures

After working with dozens of local business owners, I've seen the same three patterns. I call them fractures because they're breaks in the foundation — not surface-level problems.

🎯 Fracture #1: Avatar

You're speaking to "everyone" so you're reaching no one.

  • Posts get likes from peers, not leads from prospects
  • You attract price-shoppers and tire-kickers
  • Your message feels generic even to you

💎 Fracture #2: Offer

You're selling what you do (the process) instead of the transformation (the result).

  • Conversations stall on price
  • You compete with cheaper alternatives
  • Prospects don't understand your value until you explain it for 45 minutes

🎙️ Fracture #3: Copy

Your voice doesn't sound like you.

  • Writing feels exhausting or fake
  • You avoid posting because it doesn't feel right
  • Your content sounds like everyone else's
