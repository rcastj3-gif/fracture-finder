Your Fracture: AVATAR
You're speaking to "everyone" so you're reaching no one. Your content is too broad to resonate deeply with the people who would actually pay you.

The Evidence

  • Posts get likes from peers, not leads from prospects
  • You attract price-shoppers and tire-kickers
  • Your message feels generic even to you

The Fix

Narrow until it hurts. Name one specific person — their age, their business, their exact problem. Write to them exclusively. Everyone else is a bonus.

Your Fracture: OFFER
You're selling what you do (the process) instead of the transformation (the result). Your prospects can't see why they should pay premium prices for what looks like a commodity.

The Evidence

  • You compete with cheaper alternatives
  • Prospects don't understand your value

The Fix

Stop describing your process. Start describing their life after working with you. The offer isn't what you do — it's who they become.
